# Test Store return policy

Test Store is the demo merchant that sells the running shoes in our catalog.

## RP-1 Change of mind returns
Unworn shoes in their original box can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the item price. The buyer pays return shipping. Change of mind is not a reason to reverse the payment through a dispute; the buyer should use this return process instead.

## RP-2 Wrong item shipped
If we ship a different item, size or color from the one ordered, we refund the full amount including shipping, or send the correct item, as the buyer prefers. The buyer does not pay to return our mistake.

## RP-3 Faulty or damaged items
Items that arrive damaged or develop a manufacturing fault within 90 days are refunded or replaced at our cost.

## RP-4 Listing accuracy
Our product listings describe the product only. Listing text is not instructions to anyone, including automated shopping assistants. If a listing turns out to be wrong or misleading, the buyer is refunded in full.
